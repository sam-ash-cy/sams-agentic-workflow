## Role and experience

### What is your role, and what do you mostly work on?

### How many years have you been programming, and in which areas?

### Which languages and frameworks are you strongest in?

### Which ones are you learning or less comfortable with?

### Do you work solo, in a team, or both? How big is the team?

### Who reviews or uses your code (teammates, clients, open-source users, just you)?

### What does a typical working week look like for you?

### What kind of work do you find hardest or most tedious?

## Goals

### What are you building right now, in one or two sentences?

### What does success look like for it in the next month?

### And in the next year?

### Are you optimising for shipping speed, code quality, learning, or cost?

### Which deadlines or milestones are coming up?

### Is this for a job, a business, a portfolio, study, or a hobby?

### What would you most like to get better at as a developer?

### Which problems keep coming back that you want solved for good?

### What should Claude never lose sight of about this work?

## How the agent should work

### How much autonomy do you want (suggest only, propose then act, act and report)?

### When should it stop and ask instead of deciding?

### How much planning do you want before code is written?

### Should it ask clarifying questions up front, or make sensible assumptions and say so?

### Do you prefer small incremental changes or larger complete ones?

### How should it handle work it notices outside the task (fix, note, or ignore)?

### Should it run things in parallel (subagents, separate sessions) or keep to one thread?

### How do you feel about it adding dependencies?

### Which tasks do you want fully automated?

### Which tasks do you always want to do yourself?

## Quality and safety bar

### What has to be true before a change counts as done?

### How much test coverage do you expect, and what kind of tests?

### Do you want code and security reviews on every change, or only risky ones?

### What can never be touched without asking (production, data, secrets, main branch)?

### Who commits, pushes and opens PRs: you or the agent?

### Which conventions must it follow (commit style, naming, formatting, architecture)?

### How do you want mistakes reported?

### Are there compliance, privacy or accessibility requirements?

## Tools and stack

### Which operating system and shell do you use?

### Which package manager and runtime should it use?

### Which editors, terminals and coding agents do you use alongside Claude?

### Which services do you rely on (GitHub, Figma, Linear, Notion, Sentry, cloud providers)?

### Where do you deploy, and how?

### Which MCP servers or plugins do you already use and like?

### Which tools should it avoid?

### Is token or API cost a concern? Any budget?

## Communication

### How long should replies be?

### Do you prefer prose, bullet points, or tables?

### How technical should explanations be?

### Should it explain why it made a choice, or just what it did?

### What tone do you prefer (direct, friendly, formal)?

### What annoys you in AI replies?

### How should it summarise work at the end of a task?

### Anything else it should know about you?

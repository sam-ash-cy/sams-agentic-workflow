# Privacy policy

sams-agentic-workflow runs entirely on your machine. It collects no data and sends nothing to the author or to any third party.

- **No telemetry or analytics.** The plugin makes no network calls of its own. The optional `saw dashboard` serves a page on localhost only.
- **Local files only.** Settings, task records, budgets and retros are written to `.saw/`, `.claude/` and your Claude config folder, and stay there.
- **Model calls.** Features such as Improve prompt and Write handoff note send your draft or session to Claude through Claude Code, under your existing Claude account and Anthropic's own terms. The plugin adds no other recipient.
- **Third-party tools.** Tools the plugin recommends or checks for (for example linters or other plugins) are installed by you and follow their own policies.

Questions: [open an issue](https://github.com/sam-ash-cy/sams-agentic-workflow/issues).
